CHICAGO VISUAL REFRESH — NOVEMBER 3, 2026

History line: CHICAGO, 1952 / GRAPEVINE, 2002.
Source: https://weinbergersdeli.com/story (checked 2026-11-03).
Dan's official story states the brand was founded in Chicago in 1952 and opened
its Grapevine deli in 2002. The 1928 Italian Beef recipe date is not a founding date.
The original logo and food photography remain unaltered. The three Chicago
shortcuts reference canonical existing items: italian-beef-a3od,
chicago-dog-JTAI, and maxwell-street-polish-dog-kxOH.
